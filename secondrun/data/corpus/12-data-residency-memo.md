# Data Residency Memo — Telematics Refresh

**From:** Meridian Haulage legal and compliance
**To:** Procurement
**Date:** 2026-06-05

## The requirement

Telemetry and drivers' hours records generated by this fleet must be **processed
and stored inside the European Union**. This is not a preference. It follows
from our works council agreement and from the Annex C transfer-logging
obligations in ECTR-2026.

"System of record inside the EU" is the test. A read cache or edge node inside
the EU does not satisfy it if the authoritative copy lives elsewhere, because
the transfer has still occurred and must still be logged — and because a
subject-access or deletion request has to be satisfiable against the
authoritative copy.

## Vendor assessment

### Kestrel Telemetry — passes

System of record in Amsterdam, warm standby in Frankfurt. Both inside the EU.
No replication outside the EU, contractually committed in MSA clause 11.3.

### Orbaline — passes

System of record in Warsaw, secondary in Poznań. Both inside the EU.

### Drayfoss Mobility — passes

System of record in Stockholm, secondary in Frankfurt. Both inside the EU.

### Vantor Fleet Systems — **fails**

Vantor's system of record is in **Columbus, Ohio**, in the United States, with a
secondary site in Reno, Nevada. Vantor offers an EU edge cache in Dublin, but on
Vantor's own description the cache is populated from the US system of record and
the authoritative copy of raw telemetry and drivers' hours data remains in
**Columbus, Ohio**.

Vantor's standard terms additionally grant it the right to process customer
telemetry in the United States, which we could not accept even if the storage
question were resolved.

Vantor has stated an intention to open an EU region. An intention is not a
commitment and there is no date. We cannot contract against it.

## Conclusion

Vantor is not viable for this fleet on residency grounds alone, independently of
price, certification status or latency.
