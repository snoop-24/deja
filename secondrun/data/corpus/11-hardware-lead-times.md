# Hardware Lead Time Bulletin — Volume Orders

**Compiled by:** Meridian Haulage procurement
**Confirmed with each vendor:** week of 2026-06-15
**Basis:** a single volume order of 400 gateway units, delivered to Duisburg

Lead time is measured in calendar days from purchase order to delivery of the
full order quantity. Partial shipment does not count as delivery; procurement's
30-day window applies to the complete order.

## Confirmed lead times

| Vendor | Gateway | Lead time (400 units) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vantor Fleet Systems | V9-100 | 14 days | Held in EU distribution stock |
| Orbaline | OB-4 | 18 days | Held in Wrocław |
| **Kestrel Telemetry** | **K3-220** | **21 days** | Allocated from Eindhoven stock; Kestrel has confirmed 400 units are reserved against this tender until 2026-08-31 |
| Drayfoss Mobility | DC-77 | 62 days | Built to order in Gothenburg; no finished stock held |

## Against the requirement

The hard constraint is delivery of the complete 400-unit order within 30
calendar days of purchase order.

- Vantor at 14 days passes.
- Orbaline at 18 days passes.
- Kestrel at **21 days** passes.
- Drayfoss at 62 days **fails**, and fails by a wide margin.

## Drayfoss escalation

Procurement asked Drayfoss whether the 62-day figure could be compressed for
this order. Drayfoss confirmed in writing on 2026-06-16 that it could not commit
to anything inside 55 days at this quantity, because the DC-77 is built to order
and the current queue is full through September. Drayfoss offered a partial
shipment of 120 units at 30 days, which does not satisfy the requirement as
written.
