# 2026 Volume Pricing Schedule — Commercial Telematics

**Compiled by:** Meridian Haulage procurement, from vendor tender responses
**Compiled:** 2026-06-20
**Currency:** EUR, per unit, per month, excluding VAT

This schedule consolidates the four tender responses. It supersedes any figure
quoted in vendor marketing material.

## Base tier pricing

Tiers are applied on total contracted units. A fleet is priced entirely at the
tier its total unit count falls into — tiers are not blended.

| Vendor | 1–50 | 51–250 | **251–500** | 501–1000 | 1001+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kestrel Telemetry (FleetLink 3) | 24.50 | 19.80 | **16.90** | 15.40 | 14.20 |
| Vantor Fleet Systems (VantorOne) | 18.90 | 14.60 | **12.20** | 10.80 | 9.40 |
| Orbaline (Freight Cloud) | 22.10 | 18.30 | **15.60** | 14.10 | 13.00 |
| Drayfoss Mobility (Corridor) | 32.00 | 27.90 | **24.80** | 22.60 | 21.00 |

## Supplements

Supplements are added to the base tier rate. They are per unit, per month, and
they apply to every unit in the fleet, not only the units that trigger them.

| Supplement | Rate | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| **Cross-border corridor supplement** | **1.50** | Any fleet where more than 20% of journeys cross an international border. Charged by all four vendors at the same rate — it is a pass-through of roaming and multi-jurisdiction data handling cost. |
| Cold-climate hardware option | 0.80 | Optional. Not required for the DE/PL corridor. |
| Extended API rate limit | 2.40 | Optional. |
| Predictive maintenance module | 3.10 | Drayfoss only. Optional. |

## How to compute the rate we would actually pay

Effective rate = base tier rate at our unit count + every supplement that
applies to us.

Worked example, using a hypothetical 100-unit domestic-only fleet on Orbaline:
that fleet sits in the 51–250 tier at 18.30, and the cross-border supplement
does not apply because it is domestic-only, so the effective rate is 18.30.

Meridian's own fleet is a different case: it is a 400-unit fleet and 86% of
tractor-unit journeys cross the DE/PL border, so the cross-border corridor
supplement does apply. Do the addition for whichever vendor is recommended.

## Contract terms

All four vendors quoted 36-month terms with annual indexation capped at 3%.
Hardware is included in the per-unit monthly rate in all four cases; there is
no separate capital purchase.
