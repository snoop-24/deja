# Trade Press Roundup — European Telematics Vendors

**Source:** *Corridor Freight Monthly*, June 2026 issue, vendor supplement
**Note added by procurement:** this is a paid vendor supplement, not an
independent review. Figures here are vendor-supplied headline claims and are
**superseded by the tender responses in the 2026 volume pricing schedule.**
Retained in the corpus only because vendors quote it at us.

## Vantor Fleet Systems

"VantorOne now starts from just €9.40 per vehicle per month." Vantor's European
sales lead told the supplement that the company is "aggressively expanding in
Europe" and described an EU hosting region as "on the roadmap." Vantor also
promotes what it calls "full European compliance readiness," which on enquiry
refers to its domestic-annex certification.

## Kestrel Telemetry

Kestrel took a quieter position in the supplement, focusing on its
cross-border segment tagging and its long-term-support firmware branch. No
headline price was given. Kestrel's marketing director is quoted saying the
company "would rather be second on price and first on the register."

## Orbaline

Orbaline promoted its Polish-language driver app and its Central European
coverage. The supplement does not mention the March platform incident. Orbaline
describes its availability record as "industry standard."

## Drayfoss Mobility

Drayfoss promoted the predictive maintenance module and its dead-reckoning
positioning. The supplement describes Drayfoss as "the data quality leader" and
does not discuss price or lead time.

## Editorial commentary

The supplement's own editorial concludes that "buyers should expect the four
platforms to converge on capability within eighteen months, at which point price
becomes the only differentiator." Procurement does not endorse this view; it
ignores certification scope, residency and delivery, all of which are live
differentiators today.
